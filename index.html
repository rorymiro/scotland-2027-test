Scotland 2027

Initial creative ideas — Friday Welcome Party

This initial set of ideas is intended as a creative starting point rather than a finished recommendation set. Artists have been grouped into a series of creative directions to explore — from contemporary orchestral and folk collaborations to vocal ensembles. The ambition is to work with Scotland's finest artists, composers and collaborators to create entertainment that feels authentically Scottish, unique to this wedding, and perfectly suited to the couple and the setting.

Friday — Welcome Party

Set in a Scottish National Park

A major creative opportunity of the weekend — a standout performance that could become the focal point of the evening. Open to one headline performance, or a combination of an arrival performance followed by a main act. A performance that should feel atmospheric, elegant, and rooted in a sense of place.

Creative Direction 01

Contemporary Artist with Renowned Scottish Orchestra

A Scottish orchestra collaborating with a contemporary artist, or staging a showcase with an exciting modern twist — pairing the scale and prestige of a full orchestra with a genuinely current, crowd-facing sound.

Royal Scottish National Orchestra
Scottish Chamber Orchestra
BBC Scottish Symphony Orchestra

→ collaborating with

01

Lewis Capaldi

Epic Scottish singer-songwriter blending stripped-back piano ballads with soaring anthems. One of the UK's biggest global artists, with multiple No.1 albums and singles, combining an extraordinary connection with audiences and songs that have resonated around the world.

02

Paolo Nutini

Scottish singer-songwriter celebrated for his unmistakable voice and genre-spanning sound, blending soul, rock, folk and blues. A hugely respected live performer, his music feels both deeply personal and effortlessly timeless.

03

Travis

Iconic Scottish alt-rock band whose melodic songwriting helped define British guitar music in the late 90s and early 00s. Their timeless catalogue and understated style continue to influence generations of artists, including Coldplay and Keane.

Real precedent, not a stretch — the BBC Scottish Symphony Orchestra performed a full live collaboration with Travis at Glasgow's Barrowland Ballroom in 2016, broadcast on BBC One. This exact pairing already exists.
